Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted in Europe). We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate our Service.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, your personal data is removed within 30 days.

PixaMedia is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
